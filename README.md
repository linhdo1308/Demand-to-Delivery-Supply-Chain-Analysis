# From forecast to delivery: where a supply chain loses revenue and margin

All CPG company has to answer 4 biggest questions. 

1. How much will customers order? Velocity tells you what sells fast or slow, and demand forecasting predicts next weeks.
2. How much should we stock? Safety stock is set from forecast error to balance inventory cost against stockouts.
3. Did we deliver? Fill rate and OTIF show whether the stock decision worked for customers.
4. Was it worth it? Cost-to-serve shows which customers and shipping modes make or lose money, given the service you gave them.
